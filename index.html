Frequently Asked Questions

Can I donate medicines from home / hostel?
+
Yes! Simply register on MedRelief, fill the donation form with the medicine details (or use our in-browser OCR to scan the blister pack), and drop the sealed package at any of our monitored campus smart drop-boxes across campus premises. Our volunteer crew will collect it within 24 hours.
What types of medicines are accepted for donation?
+
We accept only factory-sealed, unbroken blister packs with more than 60 days of shelf life remaining. This includes common medicines like Paracetamol, Cetirizine, Metformin, Pantoprazole, and Antibiotics in original packaging. Loose pills, vials, injectables, or opened packets are strictly not accepted.
How are medicines verified before distribution?
+
Every donated medicine undergoes a mandatory 3-point clinical inspection by a licensed pharmacist: (1) Physical blister seal integrity check, (2) Expiry date validation — minimum 60 days remaining required, (3) Batch number and formulation legibility verification. Only pharmacist-approved batches enter the non-profit distribution pool.
Are the medicines sold or charged to recipients?
+
Absolutely not. MedRelief is a pure non-profit campus initiative operating under our non-profit institutional mandate. No medicine is ever sold, billed, or monetized at any point. Partner NGOs are legally bound to distribute all received stock completely free of charge to underprivileged patients.
Will I be notified about my donation's progress?
+
Yes! You will receive automated email notifications at every milestone: when your donation is registered, when our campus crew collects it, when the pharmacist verifies it, and finally when it reaches the partner NGO for free patient distribution. You can also track live status in the Student Donor portal.
Which NGOs receive the donated medicines?
+
We partner with vetted charitable organizations: Red Cross Mobile Campus Wing (emergency & first aid), Seva Rural Health Mission (diabetes & hypertension care), Samarpan Elderly Foundation (senior citizen care), and Jan Swasthya Camp Network (urban slum preventive healthcare). NGO matching is done intelligently based on the medicine's therapeutic category.

MedRelief — Campus & Community Medicine Redistribution

🌱 100% Non-Profit Initiative 🛡️ Licensed Pharmacist Verified 🤝 Verified NGO Partnerships 🔒 End-to-End Chain of Custody

MedRelief is a dedicated student-led healthcare initiative channeling surplus sealed medicines to underprivileged communities through a strict, pharmacist-inspected, zero-monetization pipeline. No medicines are ever sold or commercialized — our sole mission is eliminating medical waste and saving lives.

🎯

Mission

Eliminate campus medical waste by creating a systematic, pharmacist-verified pipeline that channels surplus sealed medicines from student donors to underprivileged communities through accredited NGO partners — with zero monetization and full clinical accountability.

🌟

Vision

A campus where every surplus medicine reaches a patient who needs it, every student donor is an agent of social change, and no sealed tablet is ever needlessly wasted when lives can be saved through thoughtful redistribution.

⚖️

Values

Clinical Safety First • Radical Transparency • Zero Commercial Interest • Pharmacist-Led Quality Control • Student Social Responsibility • NGO Partner Accountability • Environmental Stewardship.
